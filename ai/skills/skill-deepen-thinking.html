Skill: deepen-thinking

Training-layer skill: a single-session Socratic walk up the higher-order tiers (Understand → Analyze → Evaluate → Create → metacognitive close) on a subject the user already partly holds, where the user produces at every rung and the AI scaffolds and adversarially checks but never climbs a rung for them. On a request like “analyze X for me” it reframes to produce-first rather than handing over a take. The schemas being built live in the user — the foundation-paradox defense from higher-order-thinking-under-ai, applied as a ladder.

When to use

  • Wanting to think harder about a subject rather than be handed a take — “help me think about X more deeply”, “go deeper on this”, “push my thinking on X”, “think at a higher level”
  • A request to “analyze / give a deep take on” a subject you clearly hold — invoke and let it reframe to produce-first, instead of consuming an AI take
  • Building the deliberate-practice habit of producing higher-order analysis rather than offloading it

Skip when:

  • You don’t yet hold the subject — that’s a knowledge gap; use /teach (the skill routes there if rung 1 reveals this)
  • The “subject” is an in-progress plan or design with decisions to resolve — use /grill
  • You genuinely want the AI to just produce the analysis right now — answer normally; don’t force the ladder

Why it exists

The default move — ask the AI to analyze a subject and read the result — is augmentation: the analyze/evaluate/create work happens in the model, and the user’s own schemas for the subject never get built (augmented-vs-trained-thinking: who produces decides whether you trained or offloaded; higher-order-thinking-under-ai: the Evaluate tier only appreciates on schemas you actually built, and the same automation that makes it valuable cuts the reps that build it). This skill forces the inversion — the human climbs, the AI belays — and sequences the tiers explicitly because the evidence says explicit + practiced + adversarially-checked beats immersion (teaching-higher-order-thinking).
