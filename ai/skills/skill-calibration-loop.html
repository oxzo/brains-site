Skill: calibration-loop

Three-mode skill (record / resolve / review) for a personal calibration loop. Records binary predictions with explicit resolution criteria and numeric confidence, resolves them when their deadline passes, and reports Brier score and calibration buckets across sessions.

When to use

  • Making a binary prediction with a clear future resolution (deadline-bound, criterion-specifiable)
  • Predictions you’d otherwise make and forget — the ones that quietly fail to teach you anything
  • Periodic review of how your confidence sense has been doing across domains

Skip when:

  • Ephemeral guesses with no resolution criterion
  • Continuous estimates (use /reference-class for those)
  • One-off predictions you’ll never close out

Why it exists

Introspection doesn’t catch overconfidence; only scoring against outcomes does. Confident-sounding judgments that never get resolved let the internal probability sense drift uncorrected — and overconfidence in particular is the failure mode least visible from inside. Brier score and calibration buckets convert subjective confidence into a measurable signal, broken down by domain so blind spots become locatable.
