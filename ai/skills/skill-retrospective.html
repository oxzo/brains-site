Skill: retrospective

Run a learning retrospective on a completed development cycle, project phase, or substantial working session. The skill separates decision-quality from outcome-quality (Duke’s “resulting”), extracts transferable lessons phrased for reuse, and auto-appends any resolvable or new prediction to journal/calibration-log.md. It is the ⑦ stage and the only genuinely generative skill of the intent-to-retrospective loop — it produces lessons and scored predictions rather than evaluating an artifact.

When to use

  • At the close of a development cycle, project, or phase that began from a stated intent
  • At the end of a substantial working session that made real decisions
  • Before reusing a past outcome as evidence — to check the “lesson” isn’t a resulting artifact
  • Periodically, to keep the calibration loop fed (the retro is the main inflow to journal/calibration-log.md)

Skip when:

  • The cycle is mid-flight, before outcomes are known
  • The session was trivial or purely mechanical (refuse-to-retro)
  • You only need accounting (use /commitment-ledger)
  • You’re resolving or scoring existing predictions (use /calibration-loop resolve / review)

Why it exists

Two failure modes. Resulting (Annie Duke, Thinking in Bets, 2018) — judging a decision by how it turned out rather than by the process given what was knowable — makes a cycle’s “lessons” encode luck as validated process; the resulting 2×2 forces the two axes apart and flags the dangerous quadrants (good-outcome/bad-process must not graduate to a repeatable lesson; bad-outcome/good-process must not trigger a recant). Hindsight + evaporation — what-we-learned, left as prose, decays and is never tested; harvesting forward-looking claims into the calibration log turns a lesson into a scored prediction that compounds. The auto-write to journal/calibration-log.md is the design decision (2026-05-29) that makes the loop close: the retro is the calibration loop’s main inflow, which is the structural fix for the dormant-calibration problem (the log having sat at a single prediction). The skill sits in the learning layer above /commitment-ledger (accounting) and feeds /calibration-loop (scoring) — append-only, never resolving.
