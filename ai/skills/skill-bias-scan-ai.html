Skill: bias-scan-ai

Scan an AI-generated artifact (LLM-drafted note, AI research output, AI-produced code, agent-generated summary) for output-side bias fingerprints.

When to use

  • Before committing AI-drafted content to the vault, a spec, or a PR description
  • LLM-written research summaries about to be propagated into a decision
  • Agent-generated artifacts before they’re acted on

Skip when:

  • The artifact is human-authored (use /bias-scan-human — different fingerprints)
  • You’re scanning an inbound prompt, not an outbound output (use /bias-scan-prompt)
  • A targeted check is enough; /reviewers-map is the lighter-weight alternative

Why it exists

AI outputs have characteristic failure modes that human outputs don’t: hallucinated specifics, sycophancy, narrative coherence without evidence, training-data availability and survivorship bias, in-register defaults, missing failure-mode analysis. These are different in kind from human reasoning biases, so a single bias scan can’t cover both axes well. This skill operationalizes Pass 2 of the bias-aware verification protocol.
