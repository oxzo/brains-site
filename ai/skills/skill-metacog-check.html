Skill: metacog-check

Augmentation-layer orchestrator for the metacognition operation — the fourth of the four higher-order operations the appreciating-position-vs-skill session decomposed, and the gate that decides whether the other three engaged honestly. The AI self-monitors its own most recent substantive answer — calibrated confidence + a mind-changer, an inversion via /disagree, a claim map via /reviewers-map, and a competence-edge check — while the user stays the principal who decides whether to trust the self-report, because self-assessed competence is where a model is least reliable.

When to use

  • An answer is load-bearing and you want its reliability priced before acting — “how confident are you really”, “what would change your mind”, “are you sure”, “check your reasoning on that”
  • Before propagating an AI answer into a decision or an artifact
  • After a fluent, confident-sounding answer in a domain where rubber-stamping is the risk

Skip for one adversarial inversion (/disagree), a claim map of an output (/reviewers-map), bias fingerprints in an AI-authored file (/bias-scan-ai), or adversarial critique of a committed artifact (/red-team).

Why it exists

One of the four “AI performs, you stay principal” operation-skills built from the appreciating-position-vs-skill conclusion. It counters automation-bias rubber-stamping — accepting fluent output whose confidence was never priced — which higher-order-thinking-under-ai names as the characteristic failure mode of the appreciating Evaluate tier. The discriminating move is the competence-edge check (the note’s “is my schema strong enough to judge this, or am I about to rubber-stamp it?”), which no single sibling runs. The honest limit it bakes in: a model judging its own competence boundary is least reliable exactly there — so the skill reports, and the principal decides trust. The augmentation counterpart to /deepen-thinking’s metacognitive close.
