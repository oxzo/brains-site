Skill: skill-quality-audit

Deep structural audit of one skill’s SKILL.md — body, structure, output contract, and metadata conformance. The complement of skill-trigger-audit: that skill asks “will it fire on the right queries”, this one asks “when it fires, is it built to produce reliable output”.

When to use

  • Before publishing a new skill to .claude/skills/
  • After substantially editing a skill body
  • When a skill triggers correctly but its output is weak, wandering, or unverifiable
  • As the per-skill lens in a catalog-wide review (fan-out, one audit per skill)

Skip when:

  • The concern is retrieval matching (use /skill-trigger-audit) or prose density (use /density-scan)
  • The target is a bundled built-in skill the user can’t modify
  • The artifact is a non-skill prompt or agent brief (/context-budget-scan, /bias-scan-prompt)

Why it exists

The catalog had a trigger-axis instrument but nothing auditing the body — the procedure, the output contract, the truthfulness of the description against what the body delivers. A skill can fire perfectly and still underperform: an unreachable clean verdict turns a scan into a noise generator, an unbounded procedure makes two runs diverge, an over-1024-char description gets its routing clauses truncated out of the router’s view. Nine axes, severity traced to the verification labels in skill-quality-criteria ([V] primary-source-confirmed / [U] community practice / [O] locally observed), verdicts Sound / Tune / Restructure with Sound defined as reachable.

Built 2026-06-05 from a deep-research pass distilled into skill-quality-criteria; draft was red-teamed (three findings fixed: mis-specified reference-note path, unearned verification labels on axes 8–9, unreachable Sound verdict) and trigger-audited (Trigger-clean) before commit.
