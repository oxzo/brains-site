Skill: claim-function-scan

Triage the load-bearing claims in a draft note, decision, strategy doc, or organizational artifact into three categories: empirical (truth-dependent), non-empirical (coordination myth, identity marker, bureaucratic fact, narrative — legitimate but not falsifiable), or bundled (empirical claim smuggled inside a non-empirical wrapper).

When to use

  • Strategy docs, decision notes, and organizational artifacts that mix factual claims with cultural framing
  • Before /falsifiability-scan or /reviewers-map if it’s unclear which claims are even in scope for falsifiability
  • Any artifact where some claims feel “immune to challenge” and you want to test why

Skip when:

  • Pure technical artifacts (no narrative wrapper to bundle into)
  • Pure narrative or values statements (no empirical claims to extract)

Why it exists

The actionable failure mode isn’t a false claim — it’s a true-or-false empirical claim wrapped in an identity, mission, or coordination frame, which makes it socially unchallengeable. Splitting bundled claims into their empirical and non-empirical halves restores both halves to the right epistemic regime: the empirical part can be tested, the non-empirical part can be honored without pretending it’s evidence.
