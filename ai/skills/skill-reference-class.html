Skill: reference-class

Apply outside-view / reference-class forecasting to the quantitative estimates in a draft plan, ADR, prediction, or scoping note. For each estimate, identify a reference class of comparable past cases, build the distribution, and locate the inside-view estimate against it.

When to use

  • Artifacts containing numeric predictions: durations, costs, sizes, throughput, percentages
  • Plans where the estimates are the load-bearing claim (scoping, project sizing, ADRs)
  • Predictions about counts or magnitudes rather than binary outcomes

Skip when:

  • Estimates that are obviously intuitions, not commitments
  • Binary predictions (use /calibration-loop for those)
  • One-off magnitude calls where there’s no useful reference class

Why it exists

Bottom-up estimation is systematically optimistic — the planning fallacy is well-replicated and not curable by trying harder. Outside-view forecasting locates the inside-view estimate against a distribution of comparable past cases, converting “feels reasonable” into “lies at the X percentile of similar work.” That’s the only correction known to actually shift estimates closer to outcome.
