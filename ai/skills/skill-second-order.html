Skill: second-order

Neutral forward simulation of a proposed action’s consequence cascade across three orders, with named actors and time horizons. Produces a sign-neutral map (1st → 2nd → 3rd) surfacing both favorable and unfavorable branches without filtering, and flags speculation creep.

When to use

  • Before committing to an action whose first-order effect is the only effect that’s been examined
  • Policy decisions, system changes, or interventions with named stakeholders
  • When the proposal feels obviously good — that’s the System-1 closure signal this skill is for

Skip when:

  • Actions with single, contained effects (no second-order surface)
  • Reversible local changes where forward simulation costs more than re-doing

Why it exists

System-1 stops at the first plausibly-good consequence and treats deliberation as finished. Forced three-order simulation with named actors and time horizons surfaces favorable AND unfavorable downstream branches without filtering, before commitment. Pairs with /red-team (which weaponizes the unfavorable branches) and /reference-class (which base-rates the load-bearing links).
