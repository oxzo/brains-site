Skill: falsifiability-scan

Scan a draft note, plan, decision, or prediction for the falsifiability shape of its load-bearing claims. Tags each claim by category (operational / deferred / vague / self-sealing / probabilistic / definitional) and produces an operationalization checklist for the ones worth sharpening.

When to use

  • Before committing artifacts that make load-bearing claims about the world (predictions, plans, decision rationale)
  • Synthesis notes where every claim sounds confident in fluent prose
  • Strategy docs where you suspect some claims have no specifiable falsifying observation

Skip when:

  • Pure narrative or instructional content
  • After /claim-function-scan already separated empirical from non-empirical claims, only re-run on the empirical half

Why it exists

Every confident claim sounds true in fluent prose. Tagging by falsifiability category makes visible which claims could be wrong and how — separating operational claims (testable) from vague, self-sealing, or definitional ones that masquerade as empirical. Without this pass, narrative coherence reads as evidence; with it, the load-bearing claims become an operationalization checklist.
