Skill: whittle-scan

Scan a queue-shaped artifact (project index, inbox, backlog, commitment ledger) for items where the cost of inaction is being underweighted because decay is invisible per week — the Whittle-indifference pattern. For each item, Claude estimates the decay mechanism and a directional subsidy (LOW / MEDIUM / HIGH); the user supplies felt urgency via AskUserQuestion. The gap between the two is the load-bearing signal — subsidy >> urgency surfaces decay the user isn’t feeling, urgency >> subsidy surfaces novelty-pull. Returns flags with implied next actions, not a full ranking.

When to use

  • A project queue, backlog, or inbox has been sitting more than a week without re-evaluation against capacity
  • Before a planning block — catch decay that “what’s urgent?” thinking won’t surface
  • After a stretch of novelty-driven work, as a check on whether novelty-pull crowded out genuinely high-cost items
  • A calibration loop, thesis-tracking artifact, or commitment ledger has resolution dates that do not fire deadline alarms

Skip when:

  • The queue has fewer than 3 items (refuse-to-scan)
  • You’re reviewing a single artifact (use /red-team or /reviewers-map)
  • The artifact is an intent statement (use /intent-check)
  • The failure mode is held-open optionality, not decay (use /option-premium-check)
  • You’re depleted — the scan is deliberation-heavy and produces noise under decision fatigue

Why it exists

Hyper-discounting and urgency illusion are the failure modes the scan counters. Intuition under-rates items whose cost compounds slowly and silently — technical debt, eroding client relationships, calibration windows that pass — because decay does not fire a deadline alarm. Symmetrically, intuition over-rates novel low-stakes items because they bid harder for prefrontal attention. The structural fix is to externalize the question: name the decay mechanism explicitly, then check whether felt urgency tracks the named cost. The Mode 3 estimator split (Claude estimates decay mechanism + subsidy; user supplies felt urgency) prevents the confabulation failure where Claude imputes urgency from artifact tone — the inverse-flag pattern would collapse under that. The restatement-frequency gate (≥ 80% restatement triggers a verbatim deferral line) prevents the skill from laundering existing roadmap text as fresh signal.
