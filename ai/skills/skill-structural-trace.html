Skill: structural-trace

Diagnose the structural behavior of a recurring pattern using stocks, flows, feedback loops, delays, Senge archetypes, and Meadows leverage points. Produces a structural map — what’s accumulating, which loops produce the observed behavior, where delays sit, where leverage actually is — not a recommendation.

When to use

  • Behavior recurs over time and the issue is the standing structure (not a discrete action)
  • “We keep doing this” / “every time we fix X, Y comes back” patterns
  • Before trying to act on a chronic problem — diagnosing structure first prevents fix-symptom errors

Skip when:

  • One-off events with no recurrence pattern
  • Clearly actor-driven failures (skill or process problems, not structural ones)
  • You need a recommendation; this skill diagnoses, doesn’t prescribe

Why it exists

Structural pathology gets blamed on actors when the loops, stocks, and delays of the system would produce the same behavior with different people in the seats. Diagnosing the structure (Senge archetypes, Meadows leverage points) before acting prevents fix-the-person errors that leave the structure intact. Pairs with /second-order (which traces a chosen action forward through the structure this skill diagnoses).
