Skill: plan-options

Generate 2–3 genuinely different candidate plans for a task or intent — each traced clause-by-clause to the intent and labelled with its tradeoff — then collapse to one with the choice reason recorded and the best ideas from the runners-up grafted in. The ② stage of the intent-to-retrospective-loop and the disciplined plan-generator that clears the ceremony bar: it forces the two disciplines the default planning pass skips — option-generation (not collapsing to the first plan that comes to mind) and intent-traceability (each plan mapped to what the intent actually asked for).

When to use

  • The start of a non-trivial cycle, after the intent is set, before committing to an approach
  • When the first plan that came to mind feels obvious — the median-collapse risk is highest exactly there
  • A decision between approaches where you want each fairly developed and traced to the goal
  • Upstream of /plan-split-scan and /branch-resolve — this picks the approach, those shape and de-risk it

Skip when:

  • The task is trivial with one obviously-right approach (generating 3 plans is ceremony — refuse, and say so)
  • The approach is already constrained or decided
  • You want to audit an already-drafted plan’s size (use /plan-split-scan)
  • You’re doing open-ended ideation not aimed at a set intent (use /scamper, /assumption-reversal)

Why it exists

Median-collapse is the failure mode it counters: the default planning pass silently settles on the most training-data-typical approach without generating alternatives or checking any against the actual intent. A generative planning skill only earns its slot if it forces a discipline the default skips — so this one is built on exactly that bar (option-generation + intent-traceability), and it self-guards against the inverse failure (refuse on trivial tasks where three plans would be ceremony). The collapse step is as important as the divergence: generating options without choosing is brainstorming, not planning, so the skill converges on one plan with a recorded reason and grafts the runners-up’s best ideas in. It hands the chosen plan downstream to /plan-split-scan (right size?) and /branch-resolve (unresolved branches?).
